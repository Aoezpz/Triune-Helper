Rebrand

Five directions

Dropping Triune entirely, and dropping the palette with it — the current colours were lifted from PTDex's stylesheet, so keeping them would only be half a rebrand. Each direction below carries its own name, mark, type and ground.

Nexus Reader Emu Multitool your art

Answers both open questions: no server in the name, and “Reader” states the app's whole promise. The key art stays as a banner; the icon has to be a flat mark derived from it — see direction E for that and the two other things to fix.

Emu Buddy
Mascot · warm · playful

Leans all the way into the pun. An actual bird you'd recognise on a Discord avatar.

Risk: cute can undercut a data tool.
Emu
Reduced · technical · quiet

Same joke, said once and dropped. The bird survives as a single stroke; the tool does the talking.

Risk: one syllable is hard to search for.
Roost
Warm · metaphor · ownable

Three eggs in a nest — your three classes, in the same colours the meter already gives them.

Risk: no emu, so the pun is gone.
Trilog
Trio-first · precise · sober

Trio plus log, and nothing else. Says what it reads and how many of them, with no mascot to age.

Risk: reads as a logging library.

The three egg colours in Roost and the meter's per-character colours are the same set on purpose — sky, amber, orchid stay separable under red-green colour blindness, which is why they were chosen. Those stay whichever name wins; only the brand tokens change.
