Nexus Reader
Emu Multitool

The name does two things the other four could not. Emu Multitool says server-agnostic without naming a server — and Reader happens to state the app's whole promise, which is that it only ever reads. That is worth more than the pun was.

What has to change before it ships
1 It cannot be the app icon. Hundreds of faceted shards, glow and rune columns become a violet smudge at 16px, which is the size it lives at in the taskbar and the Start menu. It needs a flat mark derived from it — below.
2 The lettering is art, not type. Bevelled and gradient-filled, it cannot be set at 15px in a title bar. Cinzel carries the same inscriptional feel and is a real font.
3 The glow stays out of the data. The app already holds that line — chrome may shimmer, damage rows may not. Twelve glowing bars are twelve unreadable bars.
